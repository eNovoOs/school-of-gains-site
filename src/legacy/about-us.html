Stay Informed

A Word From The Team...

We started as a small personal server with a handful of traders sharing ideas and strategies. What began as casual conversation quickly evolved into something bigger—a community united by a single mission: cutting through the noise.

We realized early on that the retail trading space was flooded with empty promises and get-rich-quick schemes. Influencers selling signals, gurus with no skin in the game, and communities built on hype rather than education. We knew we had to fight back.

Growth is mandatory for survival. Today, we've built a thriving community of 2,500+ traders who believe in the power of institutional-level education and genuine mentorship. Every day, we're working to redefine what a trading community should be.

The School of Gains Difference

We don't sell Lamborghinis. We don't promise overnight riches. We don't hype every trade or celebrate lucky wins. Instead, we focus on what actually works: disciplined education, proven systems, and a community built on integrity.

Our approach centers on institutional-grade analysis, risk management protocols, and the psychological fortitude required to trade consistently. Whether you're new to markets or have years of experience, School of Gains provides the frameworks and mentorship you need to evolve as a trader.

We measure success not by flashy screenshots, but by member growth, educational impact, and the long-term wealth creation of our community.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes the School of Gains Discord different from other trading groups?
⌄

School of Gains focuses on institutional-level education over hype. Our community is built around transparency, risk management, and long-term wealth creation. Unlike typical Discord trading servers that focus on quick signal calls, we teach you how to build your own trading edge. You'll learn from experienced traders sharing real chart analysis, entry/exit strategies, and the psychology required for consistent success.

Do I need to be an experienced trader to join?
⌄

Not at all. School of Gains welcomes traders of all experience levels. Whether you're brand new to the markets or have been trading for years, our community has resources for you. Beginners benefit from our comprehensive educational materials and mentorship from seasoned traders. Experienced traders appreciate the advanced strategy discussions and professional-grade analysis. The Discord has dedicated channels for different skill levels, so you'll always find content relevant to your journey.

What do I get with the free vs. premium access?
⌄

Free members get access to community channels, market news updates, general support, and basic resources. Premium members unlock live trading sessions with Jason, detailed trade breakdowns, educational mini-lessons, access to our full educational archive, and advanced strategy discussions. Premium is designed for those who want live mentorship and deeper access to our most valuable content. Many traders start with free access and upgrade when they're ready for intensive coaching.

What time are the live sessions and how do I join?
⌄

We run live sessions daily: Morning Market Prep at 9:00 AM EST and Afternoon Recap at 4:15 PM EST. These sessions feature real-time chart analysis, trade setups, and community discussion. Premium members can join these sessions live in the Discord voice channel. If you can't make the live session, all recordings are saved in our archive for premium members to review anytime.

Can I ask Jason questions directly during live sessions?
⌄

Yes. During live sessions, Jason and the trading team actively engage with the community. You can ask chart-related questions, seek clarification on strategies, or discuss market observations in the live chat. The sessions are designed as interactive learning experiences, not just one-way presentations. This direct access to experienced traders is one of the biggest advantages of being part of our community.

What kind of educational resources are available in the Discord?
⌄

Our Resource Vault includes custom trading indicators, TradingView scripts, chart pattern references, entry/exit templates, and more. We also provide access to custom scanners for daily, weekly, and swing trade setups. The vault is continuously updated with new tools and resources as we discover and develop them. Additionally, we document all live trades with detailed breakdowns explaining our reasoning, entry points, targets, and risk management. It's like having a trading education degree compressed into a growing library of actionable content.
