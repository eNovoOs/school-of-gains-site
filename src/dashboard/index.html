Acquisition cohort funnel

One person, one acquisition cohort: the earliest recorded lead capture, quiz submission or verified booking determines entry date. The selected window filters that entry date. Later progress is counted through this report, across sales cycles without counting a person twice. Channel and campaign use the first acquisition touch saved with that entry.

Qualified means an accepted Apprentice application or a verified direct booking. It is a sales handoff measure, not a quiz score or suitability assessment. Funnel wins require a won sales cycle with a verified booking; all wins and missing-booking wins are shown separately.

Individual booking setter credit

Appointment bookings first verified in the selected window, grouped by the setter recorded on that appointment. Calendar hosts and contact owners do not establish credit. Unknown means no setter credit is recorded; administrator-attested credit is explicitly labeled. Won contacts belong to those booked sales cycles. A person booked by different setters can appear in more than one row, so contact counts must not be added across setters.

Lead intake

Contacts and capture submissions recorded within the selected window, separate from qualification quiz applications. Repeat captures count as separate submissions; one person may appear in both lead and application counts.

Period activity by channel

Applications use submission attribution. Bookings use the attribution saved with the appointment. Each appointment is counted once on its first verified booking event, including appointments later cancelled. These are period counts, not a conversion cohort.

Period activity by campaign

Applications are counted by submission date; appointments by their first verified booking date. Both use their saved latest-touch attribution. These period counts are not a conversion cohort. Empty campaign or content values appear as “(not set)”.

Booking outcomes

Current status of appointments first booked within the selected window. Each appointment is counted once; rescheduling does not add another booking. Status may change after the reporting window. Active means new or confirmed; cancelled includes cancelled or invalid appointments. Other covers unrecognized statuses.

Pipeline handoff

Only the setter and closer pipelines belong to the new process. Applications remain unbooked until an appointment is verified. These are current totals across all dates.

Data health

Booking requests being checked are awaiting verification. Requests needing review require team attention. These unresolved requests are not confirmed appointments and are not added to confirmed booking counts. Lead CRM deliveries are a subset of overall CRM deliveries. GHL lead signals are incoming native capture notifications awaiting processing or requiring review. Managed task counts show sync and review queues, not salesperson workload. All health counts are current across all dates.

Dates use UTC. This dashboard contains aggregate data only. Historical GHL records are not automatically backfilled. Zero means no recorded activity; unavailable data is shown as unavailable.
